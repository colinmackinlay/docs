The Professional Standards module enables teachers to reflect on their own practice against established standards frameworks — whether a national framework such as Australia's AITSL Teaching Standards, or a custom framework your school or Multi-Academy Trust has defined. By giving every teacher a private space to record their reflections and evidence, the module supports ongoing professional growth rather than treating standards as a one-off compliance exercise.

## What's Included

Major professional standards frameworks can be **pre-loaded** for your school, so teachers start from a structured foundation rather than building their own. Where your school or trust has developed its own descriptions of professional growth, those can be configured as your framework instead. You choose which framework is right for your context.

<Info>
  Some frameworks — for example those used in Australia — define increasing **levels of proficiency** within each standard. Teachers can use these levels to plot their own development journey over time, identifying where they currently sit and what progression looks like.
</Info>

## How It Works

Once your framework is in place, each teacher works with their own personal reflection record:

- **Access their reflection at any time** — there is no fixed schedule; teachers can return to their record whenever it is useful
- **Record evidence against each standard** — attach documents, add links, or write notes that demonstrate their practice
- **Share with a colleague or coach** — when a teacher is ready, they can share their reflection record with another staff member to support a coaching conversation, a developmental review, or a mentoring relationship

## Use Cases

The Professional Standards module supports a range of professional development activities across your school:

- **New teacher induction** — help early-career teachers understand the standards they are working towards and begin building an evidence base from day one
- **Coaching conversations** — give coaches and mentees a shared view of the standards to focus discussions on specific areas of practice
- **CPD planning** — use self-reflection records to identify development priorities and inform your school's training programme
- **MAT-wide standards adoption** — deploy a consistent framework across all schools in your trust, giving leaders a shared language for professional growth

## Key Features

- Pre-loadable national and custom professional standards frameworks
- Individual teacher access to a personal reflection record
- Evidence storage — documents, links, and written notes against each standard
- Sharing with a nominated colleague or coach for developmental conversations
