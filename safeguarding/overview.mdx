Section 11 of the Children Act 2004 places a statutory duty on schools to safeguard and promote the welfare of children. Most local authorities work with schools to meet this duty by requiring an annual safeguarding self-review — though the format varies between authorities. The Safeguarding Audit module gives you a structured, comprehensive way to carry out that review, record your evidence, and demonstrate compliance to governors, Ofsted, and your LA.

## What's Included

Clever School Systems provides a preconfigured set of safeguarding checks that combine the common core of Section 11 self-review requirements from a number of local authority audit frameworks. Because all of the included items represent sensible checks on process, most schools choose to use them all. You can add your own custom items and disable or remove any that your LA does not require.

## Roles

Three roles control access to the Safeguarding Audit module:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Safeguarding Manager** | Assigned by an Administrator. Assigns Safeguarding Owners and manages all areas and items across the audit. |
| **Safeguarding Owner** | Responsible for updating their assigned safeguarding areas and items. Receives weekly email reminders when reviews are overdue or coming up. |
| **Safeguarding Viewer** | Read-only access. Can view the full audit and print reports, but cannot make updates. |

A Safeguarding Owner assigned to an area is automatically assigned to all items within it. They can reassign individual items to other staff members, as long as those staff also hold the Safeguarding Owner (or Safeguarding Manager) role.

## Review Process

Review dates are colour-coded so you can see your safeguarding status at a glance:

- 🔴 **Red** — overdue
- 🟡 **Amber** — due within the next 4 weeks
- 🟢 **Green** — further ahead

When you mark a Safeguarding Item as **OK**, the review date advances automatically by one year. If the item is already within 4 weeks of its due date, the new review date is calculated from the original due date — keeping your annual cycle consistent and preventing it from creeping forward year on year. You can also set review dates independently, up to a maximum of 18 months ahead.

Safeguarding Owners receive a weekly email reminder covering all items that are unapproved, not OK, overdue, or due for review within the next 4 weeks.

## MAT Support

In a Multi-Academy Trust (MAT), you can designate certain Safeguarding Items as centrally managed. Only the central Safeguarding Owner can update those items, ensuring a consistent approach across all schools. Local Safeguarding Owners still receive reminders for these items so they remain informed and can contribute to the coordinated review.
