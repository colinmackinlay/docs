Regularly checking rooms and areas throughout your school building is a core part of H&S compliance. The Room Checks module lets you manage this across every space in your school — organising rooms by building or site, recording pass/fail results for each check, and tracking any actions that arise. With 28 preconfigured checks across 7 H&S areas provided for every room, you have a thorough starting point that you can customise to suit individual spaces.

## What's Included

Each room in your school comes with a menu of 28 H&S checks spanning 7 health and safety areas. Because different spaces carry different risks, you can customise which checks apply to each individual room and how often they need to be carried out.

## Organising Rooms

Organise your rooms by building or by site to reflect your school's physical layout. Set the check frequency independently for each room — so spaces with higher risk requirements can be checked more often than standard classrooms, and your schedule stays accurate and manageable.

## Recording Checks

Work through the checks for each room and record a pass or fail result for each one. If a check fails, you must record an action before you can move on — ensuring every issue is acknowledged and followed up. Safety Managers receive alerts for:

- Checks that are **pending** and not yet completed
- Checks that are **overdue**
- Rooms with **failed checks** awaiting action
