The Development Planning module helps you structure, manage, and report on school improvement plans. Whether you have a single whole-school plan or cascaded team plans, the system accommodates your approach — grouping activity into themes, assigning ownership at every level, and keeping staff on track with automated reminders.

## How It Works

Plans follow a four-level hierarchy. At the top sits the **Development Plan** itself. Below that, **Development Areas** group related activity under shared **Development Themes** you define for your school. Within each Area sit one or more **Development Initiatives** — the key pieces of work your teams are driving forward. Each Initiative then holds **Success Criteria** and **Actions** that make progress concrete and measurable.

Every level of the hierarchy — Plan, Area, Initiative, and Action — has a named **Owner**. Areas and Initiatives can also have an optional **Supporter**, a second person with the same editing rights as the Owner at that level.

## Ownership and Permissions

Owners and Supporters have editing rights to the level *below* them in the hierarchy, not the level above. For example, a Development Area Owner can edit all Initiatives and Actions within their Area, but cannot edit the Plan itself or Areas owned by someone else. This keeps accountability clear while giving team leaders genuine control over their own work.

Anyone who owns or supports any element of a plan can view and print all other plans in full. You can also assign the **Development Viewer** role to any staff member — such as a governor — to grant read-only access without editing rights.

## Actions

Actions are assigned to a named member of staff with a specific due date. The system sends automatic email reminders to keep things moving:

- **14 days before** the due date — an early reminder is sent
- **On the due date** — a reminder is sent on the day itself
- **Weekly after the due date** — reminders continue every week until the action is marked complete

Each email includes a direct link back to the Actions summary page, filtered to that specific action. From the **Actions summary page** you can view all actions across every plan in one place, filtering by status, owner, or a free-text search, and toggling completed actions in or out of the view.

## Success Criteria

Each Initiative has one or more Success Criteria that track whether the initiative is having the intended impact. Mark each criterion as one of the following:

- **Not started**
- **In progress**
- **Not met**
- **Partially met**
- **Met**

Add optional annotation **Notes** to any criterion to record context, evidence, or commentary at the time of review.

## Progress Updates

Up to three times a year you can record a termly progress update against any Initiative. These updates can be **downloaded as PDF files** for distribution, printing, or sharing with governors without requiring them to log in.

## Cascading Plans

Once you have built a whole-school plan, you can **clone** it to create team or department plans that share the same structure but can be developed for individual team priorities. For **Multi-Academy Trusts**, a central development plan can be cascaded to individual schools, ensuring common priorities are reflected across the group while each school retains the ability to add its own detail.

## Dashboard

Every staff member with access to one or more Development Plans sees a **dashboard panel** summarising the status of all Actions across all plans. Owners and Managers also see the status of the specific Actions assigned to them, giving a personalised view of what needs attention.

## Key Features

- Multi-level plan structure with themes, areas, initiatives, and actions
- Named ownership and supporters at every level of the hierarchy
- Automated email reminders for due and overdue actions
- Success criteria tracking with five status options and annotation notes
- Termly progress updates downloadable as PDF
- Clone and cascade plans to teams or individual schools
- Viewer role for governors and wider staff

<Tip>
  Governors find the **Rationale**, **Resources**, and **Monitoring** fields on each Initiative particularly useful. These fields give a high-level summary of why an initiative exists, what it requires, and how its impact will be monitored — without needing to read through every individual action.
</Tip>
