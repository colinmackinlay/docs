Risk assessment is a critical part of school safety — whether you are planning an adventurous trip, teaching in the classroom, or managing a playground. This module makes it easy to record, approve, and distribute risk assessments consistently, so every member of staff has access to the information they need, and nothing is left to chance when someone new joins the team.

## Who Can Do What

Any member of staff can create a risk assessment. Designated approvers review and approve them before they are available for wider use. All staff — unless a risk assessment is marked confidential — can read published assessments and confirm they have understood them.

## Creating a Risk Assessment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Risk Assessments list">
    Go to the Risk Assessments list and click the **"+"** button in the top bar to open the new risk assessment form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basic details">
    Complete the following fields:
    - **Activity** — the descriptive title of the risk assessment
    - **Risk Area** — select from the categories configured by your Risk Assessment Managers
    - **Location** — optional; add text to clarify where the activity takes place
    - **Owner** — defaults to you, but can be set to any member of staff
    - **Approver** — the person who will approve the assessment; set this early so it appears on their list of unapproved assessments
    - **At Risk** — select which categories of people are at risk; this helps when filtering the list and noting where control measures differ between groups
    - **Additional Information** — use this for context such as "Statutory Requirement" or references to externally commissioned assessments
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add hazards and control measures">
    Open the saved risk assessment from the list and add all relevant hazards and their associated control measures to complete the assessment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for approval">
    Once the assessment is complete, submit it to your designated approver for review and approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approvals

Once an approver reviews and approves a risk assessment, it becomes available to all staff. If the assessment is modified at any point, or is more than one year old, the approver is automatically alerted to review and re-approve it. This ensures your risk assessments stay current and accurate.

## Staff Confirmation

Staff can confirm they have read and understood individual risk assessments. If a confirmed assessment is subsequently updated, those staff members are automatically alerted and asked to re-confirm their understanding of the changes.

## Confidential Assessments

You can mark a risk assessment as confidential to restrict its visibility to only those who need to see it — for example, assessments that relate to identifiable individuals or sensitive circumstances.

<Warning>
  All staff can see risk assessments that are not marked confidential. Do not include personal information or details that identify an individual in a non-confidential risk assessment. Use the confidential flag whenever the assessment relates to a specific, identifiable person.
</Warning>

## Key Features

- Any staff member can write a risk assessment
- Designated staff approve assessments for wider use
- Changes are flagged and notifications are sent automatically
- Staff read-confirmation with re-alert on any updates
- Actions can be assigned to staff with due dates and reminders
- Confidential flag restricts visibility for sensitive assessments

<Tip>
  When you create a new risk assessment, colour-coded risk level bars on the list give you a quick visual indication of overall risk. A grey bar means no hazards have been assessed yet — add your hazards and controls to see the risk level update.
</Tip>
