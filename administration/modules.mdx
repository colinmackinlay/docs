## Enabling a Module

Follow these steps to switch on a module for your school:

1. Log in to Clever School Systems as an **Administrator**.
2. Navigate to **Administration > Modules**.
3. Locate the module you want to activate and toggle it **on**.
4. Assign at least one **Manager** role to a staff member so they can begin configuring and using the module straight away.

Repeat this process for each module you want to activate. You can disable a module at any time using the same toggle — doing so hides it from all staff until you re-enable it.

## Multi-School (MAT) Modules

If your school is part of a Multi-Academy Trust or other group, some modules can be configured centrally by a central administrator rather than managed school by school. Items owned and managed centrally remain visible to local staff, but only the central owner can create, edit, or delete them. This ensures consistency across your group while still giving individual schools full visibility of the content that applies to them.

<Tip>
  Start with the modules most critical to your regulatory compliance — such as **Compliance Audit**, **Safety Audit**, and **Safeguarding Audit** — before enabling others. Getting these in place first means your school meets its statutory obligations from day one.
</Tip>
