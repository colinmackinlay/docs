Clever School Systems supports two methods of authentication — email and password, or social media login (Google, etc.). You can use either method once your account has been created and your Administrator has linked it to your school record.

## Email and Password Login

To sign in with your email address and password:

1. Go to [https://cleverschool.systems](https://cleverschool.systems) and click **Login**.
2. Enter your registered email address and password, then submit the form.
3. If you have not yet verified your email address, check your inbox for the verification link and click it before attempting to log in.

## Social Media Login

To sign in using a social account such as Google:

1. On the login page, select the social sign-in option (for example, **Sign in with Google**).
2. Authenticate using your existing social account — no separate Clever School Systems password is required.
3. Your Administrator links your social account to your school record, so make sure they have the correct email address associated with your social provider.

## Forgotten Password

If you cannot remember your password:

1. Click the **Forgot password** link on the login page.
2. Enter your email address — you will receive a password reset link by email.
3. Click the link in the email and follow the prompts to set a new password.

Reset links expire after a short time. If your link has already expired, return to the login page and request a new one using the same **Forgot password** link.

## Account Verification

When your Administrator first adds you to the system, you receive a welcome email containing a verification link:

1. Open the welcome email and click the verification link.
2. Follow the prompts to set your password and activate your account.
3. Once activated, you can log in using either your email and password or a social account.

If you do not receive the welcome email, check your spam or junk folder. If it is not there, ask your Administrator to resend the invitation from the Users section of Administration.

<Note>
  Your login gives you access only to the modules and areas your Administrator has assigned to you. Contact your Administrator if you need access to additional parts of the system.
</Note>
