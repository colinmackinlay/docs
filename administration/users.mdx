The Users section of Clever School Systems gives Administrators full control over who has access to your school account and what they can do within it. From here you can add new staff members, adjust their roles and module assignments, temporarily suspend access, and manage accounts when staff leave — all without affecting the historical records those users created.

## Adding a User

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Administration > Users">
    From your Dashboard, navigate to **Administration** in the main menu and select **Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add User">
    Select the **Add User** button at the top of the Users list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Enter the staff member's full name and their school email address. Both fields are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign roles">
    Select the module(s) the user will have access to and assign an appropriate role within each one — for example, Compliance Viewer or Development Plans Owner. You can update roles at any time after the account has been created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Save the new user record. Clever School Systems automatically sends the staff member an email invitation with a link to set their password and activate their account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can add multiple users in sequence without leaving the Users section. Work through your staff list in one session and all invitations will be sent automatically as you save each record.
</Tip>

## User States

Every account in Clever School Systems has a status that reflects where it is in its lifecycle. You can see the current status of each user in the Users list.

### Unverified

The user has been added to the system and an invitation email has been sent, but they have not yet clicked the verification link. An unverified user cannot log in.

If a staff member has not received their invitation or it has expired, select their account and choose **Resend Verification Email** to send a fresh link.

### Active

The user has clicked their verification link, set a password, and can log in normally. Active users can access all the modules and roles assigned to them.

### Blocked

The user exists in the system but has been prevented from logging in. Use the Blocked state to suspend a staff member's access temporarily — for example, during an extended absence — without removing their account or any of the records associated with it. Restore access at any time by returning their account to Active.

### Archived

The user is no longer active at your school. Their account cannot be used to log in, but all historical records they created or contributed to remain fully intact and visible within the relevant modules. Archiving is the recommended approach when a member of staff leaves.

<Note>
  Archived users do not count towards your active user total, and their records are preserved exactly as they were. You can search for and view archived users at any time from the Users list by filtering on the Archived status.
</Note>

## Archiving and Deleting Users

When a member of staff leaves your school, you have two options for their account: **archive** it or **delete** it.

**Archiving** is the recommended approach. It marks the account as inactive and prevents the user from logging in, while keeping all records, audit trails, and contributions they made within the system fully intact. This is important for compliance, inspection evidence, and any ongoing work the user was involved in.

**Deleting** permanently removes the user account from Clever School Systems.

<Warning>
  Deleting a user account is permanent and cannot be undone. Any records directly associated with that account may also be affected. Archive users instead of deleting them unless you have a specific reason to remove the account entirely.
</Warning>

## Wonde Integration

If your school uses Wonde to connect your Management Information System (MIS) with third-party platforms, Clever School Systems can synchronise staff information directly from your MIS. This keeps user details up to date automatically and reduces manual data entry when staff join or leave.

<Note>
  Wonde integration is not enabled by default. Contact Clever School Systems support to activate this feature for your school. Once enabled, synchronisation runs automatically according to your MIS data.
</Note>
