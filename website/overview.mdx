The Website Utilities module provides two practical tools for enhancing your school website — a staff contact form system with built-in spam protection, and a word-of-the-week vocabulary display for enriching pupil literacy. Configure both tools from within Clever School Systems, then embed the relevant link or widget on your school website to make them live.

<Note>
  To display these tools on your website, you need to embed the relevant link or widget on your school's web pages. If you are unsure how to do this, your website provider or IT team can assist with adding the embed code to the correct pages.
</Note>

## Staff Contact Forms

Give visitors a safe, reliable way to contact your staff and departments directly from your website — without exposing email addresses to spam bots.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Named Staff and Department Forms" icon="address-card">
    Create individual contact forms for specific staff members or departments, so messages always reach the right person.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Spam Protection" icon="shield">
    Built-in bot detection keeps your staff inboxes clear of automated spam messages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Office Copy of Incoming Emails" icon="copy">
    Every incoming message is automatically copied to a central office address, giving you oversight of all contact received.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Replies via the School Office" icon="reply">
    Staff replies are routed via the school office, ensuring consistency and quality in all outgoing communications.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Word of the Week

Promote vocabulary enrichment by displaying a new word on your school website each week, complete with its meaning, an example sentence, and a translation into one or more languages.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Weekly Word Configuration" icon="spell-check">
    Set a different word for each week of the academic year, including its definition, an example sentence, and translations into one or two languages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Full-Year Advance Setup" icon="calendar-check">
    Configure all your words at the start of the year so the display updates automatically each week without any further input from you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Previous Words Archive" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Past words remain accessible on a dedicated archive page so pupils and parents can revisit earlier vocabulary.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to Set Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure your tools">
    Navigate to the Website Utilities section in Clever School Systems. Set up your staff contact forms and configure your word-of-the-week entries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the embed link or widget code">
    Each tool generates a link or embed code that connects your school website to the data you have configured in Clever School Systems.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the embed to your website">
    Paste the link or widget code into the relevant page on your school website. Your website provider can help you place it in the right location if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the display">
    Visit the page on your live website to confirm the contact form or word-of-the-week widget is displaying correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
