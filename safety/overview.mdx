Schools face a wide range of health and safety obligations drawn from many different sources. The Safety Audit module brings all of them together in one place, so you can monitor your school's H&S status, assign clear ownership to staff, and stay ahead of your review cycle. With 280 preconfigured requirements and good practice points across 35 categories — covering everything from accidents and e-safety to managing contractors and operating a swimming pool — you have a comprehensive starting point that you can tailor to your school.

## What's Included

Clever School Systems provides 280 preconfigured H&S checks spanning 35 categories. These are drawn from recognised requirements and good practice guidance, so most schools can use the majority of them without modification. You can configure individual checks, add your own, and disable any that don't apply to your setting.

## Roles

Three roles control access to the Safety Audit module:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Safety Manager** | Assigned by an Administrator. Assigns Safety Owners and manages all areas and items across the audit. |
| **Safety Owner** | Responsible for updating their assigned safety areas and items. Receives weekly email reminders when reviews are overdue or coming up. |
| **Safety Viewer** | Read-only access. Can view the full audit and print reports, but cannot make updates. |

A Safety Owner assigned to an area is automatically assigned to all items within it. They can reassign individual items to other staff members, as long as those staff also hold the Safety Owner (or Safety Manager) role.

## Review Cycle

Review dates are colour-coded so you can see your H&S status at a glance:

- 🔴 **Red** — overdue
- 🟡 **Amber** — due within the next 4 weeks
- 🟢 **Green** — further ahead

When you mark a Safety Item as **OK**, the review date advances automatically by one year. If the item is already within 4 weeks of its due date, the new review date is calculated from the original due date — keeping your annual cycle consistent. You can also set review dates independently, up to a maximum of 18 months ahead.

Safety Owners receive a weekly email reminder covering all items that are unapproved, not OK, overdue, or due for review within the next 4 weeks.
