While Ofsted does not require a written SEF, almost every school maintains one to reflect on strengths and areas for development. This module gives you the structure to do so efficiently, bringing together contextual statements, per-criterion evaluations, evidence links, and high-level inspection summaries in one place — so your SEF stays current, coherent, and ready to share at any time.

## What's Included

The system provides the complete structure of the Ofsted inspection framework. Evaluate your school against each criterion, record supporting evidence, and build high-level summaries for every inspection area — all without managing a complex, unwieldy document.

## Structure of Your SEF

Your SEF is organised into the following sections:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contextual Statements" icon="building-columns">
    Set the scene for your school's circumstances before the evaluative sections begin. Give inspectors — and your own leaders — the context they need to interpret your self-evaluation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Previous Inspection" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Record the priorities identified at your last inspection and document your response to them. Demonstrate how your school has acted on the feedback it received.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Development Plan Summary" icon="list-check">
    Provide a high-level summary of your current school development priorities. This section links across to your Development Plan so everything stays aligned.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Per-Area Evaluation" icon="chart-bar">
    For each Ofsted inspection area, record individual criteria, link evidence, and complete a four-field summary: **Actions Taken**, **Impact of Those Actions**, **Strengths**, and **Priorities for Development**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Outstanding Criteria" icon="star">
    Optionally evaluate your school against Outstanding descriptors and add supporting evidence for each. Use this section to build a clear case where you believe outstanding judgements apply.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Printing and Sharing

Print your SEF at any time using the built-in print output. The per-area summary pages present your four-field evaluation in a clean, readable format suited to governor meetings and inspection day itself.

<Tip>
  The one-page summary for each inspection area is particularly useful for governor meetings. Share it ahead of time so governors arrive informed and focused on the right priorities.
</Tip>

## Key Features

- Full Ofsted inspection framework structure
- Per-criterion and per-area evaluation
- Evidence hyperlinking to detailed supporting materials
- Outstanding descriptors with evidence fields
- Contextual statements section
- Previous inspection record
- Printable area summaries for governors and inspectors
