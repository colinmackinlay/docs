The Professional Development module — sometimes called Performance Management or Appraisal — manages the annual appraisal cycle for all staff, from initial objective setting through to final review. Every stage is tracked on the system, keeping both appraisers and appraisees informed and ensuring nothing slips through the gaps at the end of the year.

## The Appraisal Cycle

The process moves through four distinct phases in sequence. Both the appraiser and appraisee must agree each phase on the system before the next one begins.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Objective Setting">
    The appraiser and appraisee agree objectives together — typically three or four. You can add objectives individually, copy them from the previous year, or load them from a template for the relevant Staff Type. Each objective must include **indicative evidence or success criteria** and **professional development needs** (these can be marked as TBC or N/A if not yet known). Once both parties are satisfied, they agree the objectives on the system to close out this phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interim Review">
    Mid-year, the appraisee and appraiser meet to review progress. Either party can enter an interim comment against each objective — there is no restriction on who goes first. Once every objective has an interim comment, both parties agree the interim review on the system to mark it complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Self Review">
    At the end of the year, the appraisee writes a self-review for each objective, reflecting on their own progress and impact. When all objectives have a self-review entry, the appraisee submits it. This automatically notifies the appraiser that the review is ready for the final stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Final Review">
    The appraiser rates each objective as **Met**, **Partially Met**, or **Not Met**, then adds overall comments covering:

    - Professional standards
    - Strengths
    - Areas for development
    - Summary

    The appraisee adds a final comment in response. The appraiser then completes the review, which closes the cycle for the year.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Staff Types and Templates

Create **Staff Types** to reflect the different roles in your school — for example, Teacher, Head of Year, or Site Staff. Each Staff Type can carry a set of template objectives that pre-populate the objective-setting meeting, saving time and ensuring consistency across staff in the same role. Leaders with management responsibilities can have an additional Leadership and Management objective tailored to their area.

## Confidentiality

Appraisal records are confidential by default. Only the **appraisee** and their **appraiser** can access a review. A user assigned the **Performance Manager** role can view all reviews across the school — this is useful for managing the process and quality-assuring objectives — unless a review has been marked confidential.

The appraiser can mark any individual review as **confidential**, restricting access to just those two people. You can also mark an entire **Staff Type** as confidential — for example, senior leader appraisals — so that they are not visible to other system managers who happen to be senior leaders themselves.

<Note>
  The **Performance Manager** role is distinct from the role of **appraiser**. An appraiser is assigned to specific members of staff and can only access their own appraisees' reviews. A Performance Manager has a school-wide view of all non-confidential reviews for oversight and quality-assurance purposes.
</Note>

<Tip>
  You can print the review at any stage in the cycle — useful for face-to-face meetings. When the final review is complete, the system automatically emails a copy to both the appraisee and the appraiser for their records.
</Tip>
