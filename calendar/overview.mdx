School calendars repeat every year, but updating them manually is error-prone and time-consuming. The School Calendars module lets you create, manage, and publish calendars efficiently — with automatic year-on-year roll-forward and iCal subscription links so parents and staff always have the latest dates on their personal devices.

## Creating Calendars

Build one or more calendars to cover events across the school year. Organise each calendar into subsections to control exactly how much detail you share with each audience — for example, a full operational calendar for all staff, a separate view for governors, and a concise one for parents.

<Tip>
  Split your calendars by audience so each group sees only what is relevant to them. A whole-staff calendar can include internal deadlines and meetings, while a parents' calendar focuses on key dates such as term dates, parents' evenings, and school events.
</Tip>

## Publishing Calendars

Once your calendar is ready, share it using any combination of the following methods:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="iCal Links" icon="link">
    Paste the iCal link into any calendar app — Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar — so events appear automatically on personal devices and stay up to date.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email and Messaging" icon="envelope">
    Send the iCal link directly to parents and staff so they can subscribe and receive all future updates without any further action from you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Website Embedding" icon="globe">
    Add the iCal link to your school website so visitors can subscribe to your calendar with a single click.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Weekly Digest

Staff can subscribe to receive a weekly email digest of upcoming events. This keeps everyone informed without requiring them to check the calendar manually.

## Year Rollover

When a new academic year begins, carry your events forward automatically. The year rollover feature transfers existing events to future years, so you never have to recreate them from scratch.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your existing calendar">
    Check your current year's events and confirm which ones recur in the next academic year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the year rollover">
    Trigger the rollover from the School Calendars settings. Events transfer forward automatically with their dates adjusted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update and publish">
    Make any final adjustments to the rolled-forward events, then publish your new calendar and share updated iCal links with subscribers.
  </Step>
</Steps>
