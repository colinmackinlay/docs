Schools must comply with a wide range of regulations — from DfE website requirements and statutory policies to core financial regulations — and these obligations are scattered across many different sources. The Compliance Audit module brings everything together in one place, so you can monitor your school's compliance status, assign clear ownership to staff, and act before anything falls overdue.

## What's Included

Clever School Systems provides a preconfigured set of compliance checks covering statutory policies, DfE website requirements, and core financial regulations. You can enable or disable individual checks to match your school's circumstances, and add your own custom items wherever you need them.

## Roles

Three roles control access to the Compliance Audit module:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Compliance Manager** | Assigned by an Administrator. Assigns Compliance Owners and manages all areas and items across the audit. |
| **Compliance Owner** | Responsible for updating their assigned compliance areas and items. Receives weekly email reminders when reviews are overdue or coming up. |
| **Compliance Viewer** | Read-only access. Can view the full audit and print reports, but cannot make updates. |

A Compliance Owner assigned to an area is automatically assigned to all items within it. They can reassign individual items to other staff members, as long as those staff also hold the Compliance Owner (or Compliance Manager) role.

## Review Dates

Review dates are colour-coded so you can see your compliance status at a glance:

- 🔴 **Red** — overdue
- 🟡 **Amber** — due within the next 4 weeks
- 🟢 **Green** — further ahead

When you mark a Compliance Item as **OK**, the review date advances automatically by one year. If the item is already within 4 weeks of its due date, the new review date is calculated from the original due date — not the confirmation date — so the annual cycle stays consistent and doesn't creep forward over time. You can also set any review date independently, including cycles of up to 18 months.

## Multi-School Support

In a Multi-Academy Trust (MAT), you can designate certain Compliance Items as centrally managed. Only the central Compliance Owner can update those items, which reduces duplication across schools and ensures a consistent approach. Local Compliance Owners still receive reminders for these items so they remain informed and can contribute to the coordinated review.

<Tip>
  Your dashboard panel shows two views at once: the compliance items you personally own, and the overall status of the entire audit. Use this to prioritise your own actions without losing sight of the school-wide picture.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Compliance item notes are visible to all users who have access to the audit. Do not record personal, sensitive, or confidential information — including details about individual staff or pupils — in the notes fields.
