Clever School Systems is a modular school management platform that helps school staff and leaders handle the important — but often time-consuming — administrative and compliance tasks that keep a school running safely and effectively. From tracking safeguarding checks to managing staff appraisals and publishing school calendars, everything is in one place.

## Multi-school support

If your school is part of a **Multi-Academy Trust (MAT)** or local authority group, Clever School Systems supports linked school groups. Central administrators can manage shared compliance items, cascade development plans to individual schools, and maintain consistency across the group — while each school retains its own local management.

<Tip>
  Schools joining as part of a group need to be set up centrally so that they are correctly linked. Contact [support@cleverschool.systems](mailto:support@cleverschool.systems) to get your group configured.
</Tip>
