The Catholic SEF module supports Catholic schools in maintaining a structured self-evaluation against the Catholic Schools Inspection framework. Use it to reflect continuously on your school's effectiveness, keep your evidence organised, and arrive at Section 48 inspection with a clear, well-structured document ready to share.

## Structure of Your SEF

Your Catholic SEF follows the same proven structure used across the Clever School Systems SEF modules, tailored to the Catholic inspection framework:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contextual Statements" icon="building-columns">
    Capture your school's context — its community, character, and Catholic ethos — to frame the evaluative sections that follow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Previous Inspection" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Record the priorities from your last Section 48 inspection and document how your school has responded to them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Development Plan Summary" icon="list-check">
    Provide a high-level summary of your current development priorities, linking self-evaluation to your broader improvement planning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Per-Area Evaluation" icon="chart-bar">
    For each area of the Catholic inspection framework, record individual criteria, link supporting evidence, and complete the four-field summary: **Actions Taken**, **Impact of Those Actions**, **Strengths**, and **Priorities for Development**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The four-field per-area summary — Actions, Impact, Strengths, and Priorities — is well suited to governor meetings and senior leadership reviews. Print a set of area summaries ahead of any meeting where school improvement is on the agenda to keep discussion focused and evidence-led.
</Tip>

## Key Features

- Full Catholic Schools Inspection framework structure
- Per-criterion and per-area evaluation
- Evidence hyperlinking to detailed supporting materials
- Contextual statements and school context section
- Previous inspection record
- Printable per-area summaries for governors and leaders
