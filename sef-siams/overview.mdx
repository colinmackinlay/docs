The SIAMS module helps Church of England schools maintain a structured self-evaluation against the SIAMS inspection framework. Use it to reflect continuously on your school's Christian distinctiveness and effectiveness, and generate an inspection-ready print format that meets SIAMS requirements without any additional formatting work.

<Note>
  SIAMS is the inspection framework for Church of England schools, conducted under Section 48 of the Education Act 2005. SIAMS inspections are separate from Ofsted and focus specifically on the school's Christian vision and its impact across school life.
</Note>

## SIAMS-Specific Features

SIAMS inspections have particular requirements for the SEF you share at inspection: it must be a two-page summary and must not include grade evaluations. The system includes a dedicated inspection print format that meets these requirements automatically — so you can maintain your full working SEF internally while producing the correct format for inspectors with a single print action.

## Structure of Your SEF

Your SIAMS SEF follows the same logical structure as the Ofsted module, aligned to the SIAMS framework:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contextual Statements" icon="building-columns">
    Set the scene for your school's Christian context and circumstances before the evaluative sections begin.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Previous Inspection" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Record priorities from your last SIAMS inspection and document your school's response to them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Development Plan Summary" icon="list-check">
    Provide a high-level summary of current development priorities, linked to your school's broader improvement planning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Per-Area Evaluation" icon="chart-bar">
    For each SIAMS framework area, record individual criteria, link evidence, and complete the four-field summary: **Actions Taken**, **Impact of Those Actions**, **Strengths**, and **Priorities for Development**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
