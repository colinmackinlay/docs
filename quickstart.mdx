Clever School Systems gets your school up and running quickly with a short registration process, an email verification step, and a few one-time setup tasks. This page walks you through registering your school, confirming your account, adding your school identity, and inviting your first staff members — everything you need before using any of the modules.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your school">
    Go to [https://cleverschool.systems/register](https://cleverschool.systems/register) and fill in your school name, your email address, and a password of your choice. Once you submit the form, Clever School Systems creates your school account and sends you a verification email.

    <Warning>
      If your school is part of a Multi-Academy Trust (MAT), local authority group, or other federated arrangement, contact support **before** registering. Group schools must be linked centrally so that shared reporting and administration work correctly across your organisation.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate your email">
    Check your inbox for a validation email from Clever School Systems. Click the confirmation link inside to activate your account. You will be taken directly to your school Dashboard, where all your enabled modules and settings are accessible.

    <Tip>
      If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. You can also request a new verification email from the login screen.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your school logo">
    From your Dashboard, navigate to **Settings** and upload your school logo. Your logo appears on printed reports, PDF exports, and other documents generated throughout the system, giving everything a professional, branded appearance.

    <Note>
      Accepted file formats are PNG and JPEG. For the best results across all print sizes, use a high-resolution image of at least 300 px on its shortest side.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite staff">
    Go to **Administration > Users** to add other staff members to your school account. Enter each person's name and email address, then assign them an appropriate role within the modules they will use. Each invited user receives an email prompting them to set their password and access the system.

    <Tip>
      Start with your key module managers — staff who will take ownership of individual modules such as Compliance, Safeguarding, or Health and Safety. They can then assign Viewer access to additional colleagues within their own modules.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
