The Applications module helps you manage sixth form applications entirely online — gathering information from external students interested in joining your sixth form, and from internal students choosing their A-level options. Whether you are running an open evening, planning your curriculum, or preparing your timetable, this module gives you the data you need at every stage.

<Tip>
  Use the Applications module alongside the Enrolment module for a seamless journey from initial application through to full enrolment. Applicants who accept a place can move straight into the online enrolment process without duplicating their information.
</Tip>

## External Applicants

Give prospective students a straightforward way to express their interest and tell you what they would like to study. External applicants register on your site — or via a social media account — and complete a short application providing their personal details and subject preferences.

Deploy the module before and after your open events to capture interest at the peak of your marketing activity, and keep applications open so that late enquiries are never missed.

## Internal Students

Internal students do not need to provide a full application, but they can use the module to record their subject preferences. This gives your curriculum and timetabling teams the information they need to plan courses and teaching groups well ahead of the new academic year.
