The Enrolment module lets parents of new pupils complete the enrolment process entirely online — submitting personal, medical, and consent information directly to the school at their own convenience. Once parents have submitted their data, you export it as a DfE ATF file and import it straight into your Management Information System (MIS), eliminating manual data entry and the errors that come with it.

<Tip>
  You can use this module for in-year admissions as well as bulk new intake. Add students individually when a pupil joins mid-year, or upload an entire cohort in one go at the start of the admissions cycle.
</Tip>

## Adding Students

Add students to the module before you invite parents to complete the form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add students individually or in bulk">
    For in-year admissions, add students one at a time. For a new intake cohort, upload the full list in bulk to save time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send parent invitations">
    The module sends each parent an email invitation with a secure link to log in and complete the enrolment form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor submissions">
    Track which parents have submitted their data and follow up with those who have not yet responded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Importing to Your MIS

Once parents have submitted their enrolment data, export it as a DfE ATF file and import it directly into your MIS. This standard format is supported by all major MIS providers used in UK schools.

## Additional Data Capture

Beyond the core personal information, use the enrolment form to gather:

- **Medical information** — conditions, medications, allergies, and care plans
- **Trip and visit consents** — parental agreement for local and off-site activities
- **Image consent** — permission for photographs and video for school publications and social media
- **Biometric data consent** — required before using fingerprint or facial recognition systems in school

All additional data can be printed per student for filing or reference.
