The Document Management module gives you a central repository for your school's key documents — policies, guidance notes, handbooks, and more. Assign documents to staff members to require a formal read-confirmation, then monitor progress and send reminders to anyone who has not yet responded.

<Note>
  This module is particularly useful for annual policy reviews, where all staff must confirm they have read updated documents, and for new staff induction, ensuring every new joiner works through the required reading from day one.
</Note>

## Organising Documents

Group your documents into types to keep your repository easy to navigate. Common groupings include Policies, Guidance, and Handbooks, but you can configure types to match your school's own naming conventions. Upload documents directly or link to files hosted elsewhere.

## Assigning Documents to Staff

Allocate any document to one or more staff members to require their read-confirmation. When you assign a document, the relevant staff members receive a notification prompting them to read and acknowledge it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload or link your document">
    Add the document to the repository and assign it to the appropriate document type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to staff">
    Select the staff members who need to confirm they have read the document. They receive an immediate notification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor confirmations">
    View a live summary showing who has confirmed and who has not yet responded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send reminders">
    Send a reminder to any staff member who has not confirmed, directly from the monitoring view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Monitoring Confirmations

The manager view gives you a clear summary of confirmation status across all assigned documents. See at a glance who has read and acknowledged each document and who still needs to act. Send targeted reminders to outstanding staff members without leaving the module.
