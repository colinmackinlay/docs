The Orthodox SEF module provides a structured self-evaluation framework for Orthodox Christian schools, built on an adaptation of the Catholic Schools Inspection framework to reflect Orthodox values and requirements. Use it to maintain a continuous, evidence-backed self-evaluation and prepare clearly for inspection.

## Structure of Your SEF

Your Orthodox SEF follows the same structure used across the Clever School Systems SEF modules, adapted for the Orthodox inspection framework:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contextual Statements" icon="building-columns">
    Set out your school's Orthodox Christian context and ethos to frame the evaluative sections that follow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Previous Inspection" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Record priorities identified at your last inspection and document how your school has responded to them.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Development Plan Summary" icon="list-check">
    Provide a high-level summary of your current development priorities, linking self-evaluation to your wider improvement planning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Per-Area Evaluation" icon="chart-bar">
    For each framework area, record individual criteria, link supporting evidence, and complete the four-field summary: **Actions Taken**, **Impact of Those Actions**, **Strengths**, and **Priorities for Development**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  If you are unsure which SEF module applies to your school, contact [support@cleverschool.systems](mailto:support@cleverschool.systems) for guidance.
</Info>

## Key Features

- Orthodox-adapted inspection framework structure
- Per-criterion and per-area evaluation
- Evidence hyperlinking to detailed supporting materials
- Contextual statements section
- Previous inspection record
- Printable per-area summaries for governors and leaders
