A risk register is a strategic document for recording the risks facing your school and how you are managing them. This module gives you a simple, structured way to maintain your risk register and present it clearly to governors, keeping the focus on what matters without unnecessary complexity.

## Recording a Risk

When you add a risk to the register, complete the following fields to build a clear picture of both the raw risk and its managed level:

- **Likelihood** and **Impact** (before treatment) — assess these to calculate an initial risk level, reflecting the risk as it stands before any controls are in place
- **Treatment approach** — choose how you intend to manage the risk:
  - **Treat** *(default)* — take action to reduce the likelihood or impact
  - **Tolerate** — accept the risk as it stands
  - **Transfer** — pass the risk to a third party, such as through insurance
  - **Terminate** — eliminate the activity or situation giving rise to the risk
- **Control measures** — record the steps you are taking to manage the risk
- **Post-treatment likelihood and impact** — reassess likelihood and impact after your controls are in place to show the residual risk level
- **Additional notes** — add context for governors and reviewers, particularly when a risk level has changed or a risk is newly identified

## Organising Risks

Group your risks by category to make the register easier to read and navigate. Your Risk Register Manager can configure the available categories to match your school's needs.

## Reporting

Print the risk register as a PDF to share with governors or to keep as a formal record. The PDF format presents the register clearly and is suitable for use in governor meetings and for compliance purposes.

<Tip>
  Record notes separately from your control measures to keep the register succinct and readable. Notes are especially useful when a risk level has changed or when a new risk has been added — they give governors the context they need without cluttering the controls section.
</Tip>

## Key Features

- Likelihood and impact scoring before and after treatment
- Treatment strategy classification: Treat, Tolerate, Transfer, or Terminate
- Control measures recorded against each risk
- Residual risk assessment after controls are applied
- Categorisation for clear organisation and filtering
- PDF export for governors and record keeping
